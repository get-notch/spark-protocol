## What emerged

1. **The ideate intent** — a working divergent thinking mode for Claude Code
2. **The /spark skill** — 3-phase ping-pong protocol with chain capture
3. **The /fomo skill** — zero-friction idea capture
4. **The dream bridge** — background cross-referencing between sessions
5. **The neuroscience mapping** — DMN/ECN/SN → protocol architecture
6. **The Mythos parallel** — structural similarity between this protocol's design and Anthropic's leaked Mythos description
7. **The nuclear fission model** — chain sustaining mechanics
8. **The V&V framework** — validation strategy for creative systems
9. **This repo** — open-source release of the entire system

All from one anchor: "I want an ideate mode."

## Why this chain worked

- **Hop 5** was the turning point — I described HOW my brain sparks, not just WHAT I wanted. The protocol was designed for the cognitive reality, not an abstraction.
- **Hop 6** was the reframe — "teach Claude to spark" instead of "capture sparks." Changed the entire design direction.
- **Hop 12a** was the fork that made it research — connecting ADHD to AGI wasn't planned, it emerged.
- **Hop 20-21** was unplanned — a news event mid-session surfaced a structural parallel with the architecture we'd already built. The chain ended naturally after that.

The chain demonstrates exactly what the spark protocol is designed to produce: non-obvious connections that emerge from ping-pong divergent thinking, not from structured planning.
